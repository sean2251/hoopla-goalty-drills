3v3 w/ screen - Week2

  1. (1)C circles around the outside of the goal to the Center spot at the top. PF & G wait until C is almost in position.
  2. (2)PF makes a free pass to G.
  3. (3)PF cuts straight into the goal.
  4. (4)If PF is open on the cut, G throws it for the goal.
  5. (5)If not, PF keeps going and sets a screen on C's defender.
  6. (6)C runs off the screen to the front left of the goal.
  7. (7)PF rolls off the screen to the front right (screen and roll).
  8. (8)G throws to whichever cutter is open.
  9. (9)Rotate: All three pairs move together to the back of the next position's line. C -> PF; PF -> G; G->C. Lines are mixed genders but pairs stay the same throughout.

Note: Unlimited stall count on the mark. The first pass is free and the Center's defender has to stay on their right side through the screen. Otherwise all three defenders play live. Labels show the role each player ends up in.

Drill Goals

  1. 1.Practice throw & gos (learn what being "open" looks like)
  2. 2.Practice setting screens on the back

Drill Modifications

  1. 1.Start with no defenders until the timing is down.
  2. 2.Switch sides so C comes off to the front right and PF rolls front left (good idea to switch up pairs of people while doing this too).

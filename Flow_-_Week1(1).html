Flow - Week1

  1. (1)Disc starts with SF who throws it to the G
  2. (2)SF immediately cuts to power position
  3. (3)G throws back to SF
  4. (4)As SF is getting the disc, PF should be cutting across to give an option at the front right cone
  5. (5)As PF is cutting across, C cuts down to the front left cone
  6. (6)SF throws to C for goal
  7. (7)PF → C · C → back of line · G → PF · SF → G · next in line → SF

Drill Goals

  1. 1.Learn the basic positions, positioning, and spacing (keep the goal clear!!!)
  2. 2.Get the disc to and take shots from power position (right in front of the goal)
  3. 3.Throw and go always
  4. 4.We can attack the goal with horizontal and vertical cuts

Drill Modifications

  1. 1.Should switch sides about halfway through
  2. 2.Next steps are the PF and C adding change of direction moves to their cutting. Also allowing the SF to choose who they throw to.

3v3 w/ pick - Week2

  1. (1)C circles around the outside of the goal to the Center spot at the top. PF & G wait until C is almost in position.
  2. (2)PF makes a free pass to G.
  3. (3)PF cuts straight into the goal.
  4. (4)If PF is open on the cut, G throws it for the goal.
  5. (5)If not, PF keeps going and sets a pick on C's defender.
  6. (6)C runs off the pick to the front left of the goal.
  7. (7)PF rolls off the pick to the front right (pick and roll).
  8. (8)G throws to whichever cutter is open.
  9. (9)Rotate: the next pair in line steps in as PF; PF → C; C → G; G goes to the back of their own line. The MMP and WMP lines take turns sending in the next pair. Each defender moves with their player.

Note: Unlimited stall count on the mark. The first pass is free; after that all three defenders play live. Labels show the role each player ends up in.

Drill Goals

  1. 1.Practice throw & gos (learn what being "open" looks like)
  2. 2.Practice setting picks on the back

Drill Modifications

  1. 1.Start with no defenders until the timing is down.
  2. 2.Switch sides so C comes off to the front right and PF rolls front left.
